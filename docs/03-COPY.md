## The voice

Warm, plain, specific. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. The confidence comes from detail, not adjectives — a senior designer says *"sponsor logos sit inside the avatar, so the badge couldn't be centered"* where a junior says *"I'm passionate about user-centered design."*

Friendly means **human**, not cute-in-the-writing. Let the interface be playful; keep the prose direct. One or two dry jokes across the whole site is the right dose. Your own line — *"moving fast shouldn't mean losing the human parts"* — is exactly the register.

**Cut forever:** passionate, seamless, cutting-edge, leverage, holistic, delightful, "available on request."
